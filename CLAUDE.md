## Read before you start

- `docs/LESSONS.md`: fixes and gotchas from earlier sessions. Add an entry before handing back
  whenever you fixed something non-obvious or lost time to a trap.
- `docs/TAVERN_STYLE.md`: the Walnut Tavern style (colours, materials, frames, type, controls,
  sheet layouts). Read it before building or porting any screen; Android screens follow it.
- `docs/ANDROID_PARITY.md` for Android work, `DESIGN.md` for general design principles.
