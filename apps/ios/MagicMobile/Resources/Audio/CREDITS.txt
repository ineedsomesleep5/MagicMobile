MagicMobile sound effects and music

Recorded foley (card handling, dice, interface clicks, glass, metal, sword, page, impacts)
comes from these Kenney.nl packs, released under Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0,
public domain, http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Credit is not
required; thank you, Kenney (www.kenney.nl):
  Casino Audio, Interface Sounds, RPG Audio, Impact Sounds.

All spell, bell, fanfare, stinger and music material is original synthesis made for
MagicMobile (scripts/audio/build_game_audio.py), layered with the foley above.
