MagicMobile

Privacy

Last updated October 24, 2026. Applies to the MagicMobile apps for iPhone and Android, the browser game and this website.

MagicMobile is an independent, non-commercial fan project. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking in the apps, the browser game or on this site, and nothing is sold or shared for advertising.

On your device

Your decks, game settings, a saved game you can resume, downloaded card art, and the app's own crash and engine reports are kept on your device. Games against the AI run entirely on your device, in the browser too. Crash reports are only shared if you choose to send one from the app. The app also notes when a game freezes; if you tap Send on its question, or Report a freeze in the game menu, the report (the game’s state at that moment, your recent moves, your app version, phone model and memory use, not your decks or account details) is stored with your account ID in MagicMobile’s database so the freeze can be fixed.

The browser game keeps the same kind of things in your browser's storage for this site: your decks and settings, your profile's sign-in session, and finished games waiting to upload. It also keeps the game engine in the browser's cache so it only downloads once. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes them.

Profiles and friends

On a phone, opening Friends creates an anonymous account (no email, no password); the browser game creates one when you first use it. MagicMobile's database, hosted by Supabase in the United States, then stores:

  • a random account ID and the player name you choose;
  • your friends and friend requests, players you block, and messages with your friends;
  • when you were last online, your phone type (iPhone or Android; the browser game sends none), and the code of a table you are hosting while it has open seats. Only your accepted friends can see these;
  • reports you send about another player, including the reported message.

Your decks on every device

Once you have a profile, your saved decks are also stored with it, so they show up on every phone and browser signed in to the same profile: each deck's name, commander, card list, chosen printings and where it was imported from. A deck you delete is emptied on the server and removed from your other devices.

Signing in with Apple or Google

Signing in is optional. It links your profile to your Apple or Google account so it follows you to another phone or browser (the browser game offers Google). Supabase stores the account identifier and the email address Apple or Google shares with us (Apple may give a private relay address instead). It is used only to sign you in, is shown only to you, and is never shared with other players.

Your profile and game history

Once you choose a player name, MagicMobile also stores:

  • your finished games: when you played, the mode, the result, your deck's name, commanders and colors, your opponents' names and commanders, the number of turns and how long it took. The latest 500 games are kept;
  • your ranked standing: rank, points, wins and losses this season and past seasons, and the result of each ranked game, including ranked games against the AI, so the server can keep score fairly;
  • your title and favorite commander, if you pick them.

Your profile and game history are public by default so other players can find you. You can make them friends-only or private on your Profile, under Who can see your profile. Players you block can't see them either way.

You can delete all of this at any time: Friends → Delete my profile removes your account, profile, decks, game history and ranked standing. Reports you sent are kept without your account attached so abuse can still be reviewed.

Online tables and chat

Online tables pass game moves and chat messages between players' devices through a relay on Cloudflare. The relay keeps a table's roster and undelivered messages only while the table is open and deletes them when everyone leaves or after 30 idle minutes. Chat is not stored on a server unless someone reports a message. Game Center tables use Apple's Game Center.

Card images and deck imports

With Scryfall live images on (the default on phones; you can turn it off in Settings), the app requests card images from Scryfall, which receives the card names being shown, including cards in your hand, and your IP address. Downloaded images then work offline. The browser game always loads card images from Scryfall; its mana symbols and card back come from the site itself.

When you import a deck from Archidekt or Moxfield in the browser game, the request goes through the game's own address on Vercel, which fetches the deck link you entered.

This website and the browser game

Both are hosted on Vercel, which receives your requests, including your IP address, in order to serve them. On networks that block Supabase, the browser game reaches MagicMobile's database through its own address on Vercel, which passes the requests on.

Children

MagicMobile is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.
