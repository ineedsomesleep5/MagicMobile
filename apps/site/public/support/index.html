Support MagicMobile

MagicMobile is free, with no ads and nothing to buy in the app. If you enjoy it, a small tip helps cover the cost of running it.

Leave a tip ↗

Opens Ko-fi. Tips are optional and unlock nothing in the game.

What tips pay for

  • the database behind profiles, friends and ranked games;
  • the relay that connects iPhone and Android players at online tables;
  • the yearly Apple developer membership that keeps TestFlight builds coming.

Other ways to help

Tell a friend, bring your playgroup to an online table, or report a bug on the project's GitHub page.
