## Part 1: engine size (docs/cloud/engine-size.md)

- **Measured what fills the 202 MB of code**, from the unstripped Android library's symbol table (GraalVM
  22.1 has no build report, and cross-compiles exit before printing the usual breakdown): card classes
  117.7 MB (58%), set classes 27.7 MB (14%), the generated card factory 18.7 MB (9%), JDK 11.2 MB (TLS and
  crypto about 3.5 MB), H2 3.2 MB, protobuf 1.3 MB. New workflow `magicmobile-native-code-sizes.yml` and
  script `scripts/native_code_sizes.py`; runs about a minute and builds nothing.
- **Found 24 MB of run-time heap** (JVM measurement): the set registry installed at engine start keeps
  93,420 `SetCardInfo` objects, data the embedded catalogue already has.
- **Implemented one cut**: a table-driven generated card factory (estimated 8–15 MB of code). Same
  constructor for all 32,498 catalogue classes, checked against the old factory.
- **Proposed** (not implemented, need a native build to confirm): sets from the catalogue (up to 27.7 MB
  code and about 24 MB heap), H2 and ORMLite JDBC unreachable (about 3.5 MB), no TLS (up to 3.5 MB),
  protobuf out of checkpoint registration (up to 1.3 MB), locales and charsets.
- **Opt-in heap report** for the next Android native build: `native_reports` input on
  `magicmobile-android.yml` (`MM_NATIVE_REPORTS=1` in `build_native.sh`). Off by default.

## Part 2: stronger AI at skill 5 and 6 (in progress)

- **Finding: the node cap is not what limits the search.** Search stats from calibration games: searches
  use a median of 12–18 nodes (90th percentile about 600) against XMage's 5000 cap; a node is a full
  Commander game copy, about 20 ms. Time runs out long before nodes, and attacks and blocks don't use the
  search at all (fixed upstream rules), so raising the cap would change nothing.
- **Expert tier (skill 5 and 6)**, `AIDifficulty.expert`:
  - `CombatPlanner`: picks attacks against a predicted defender and the crack-back on the next turn
    (combat arithmetic on the real permanents, upstream's own life and permanent values), and chump-blocks
    when the unblocked attack would be lethal.
  - A quick two-ply search (at most 2 s) before the full one: when the full search runs out of time
    before scoring any move, the quick move stands instead of a pass. Total think time stays within
    `skill × 3` s.
  - Blocks from an optimizer that judges the whole attack at once (single and pair moves, so double
    blocks and menace), on attackers killed, blockers lost and the life change.
  - **Life on the game's scale.** Upstream's life score values a point at 100 above 20 life (tuned for
    20-life formats). At Commander's 40 the AI let five 5/3s through and fell from 40 to 15, then lost
    (replay of calibration game 32). Expert attacks and blocks score life on upstream's curve scaled to
    the starting life (identical at 20).
- Harness fix: calibration seats now go through the production seat parser, so they get every skill
  setting (they built `AIDifficulty` themselves and missed the expert flag).
